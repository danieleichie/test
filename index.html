About these rates

These are daily global reference rates, not live trading prices. Banks, bureaus de change, and the parallel market in Nigeria can charge different rates, so check with your provider before you send or exchange money.

The live dollar rate is an estimate built from the Quidax exchange price and advertised peer-to-peer offers on Binance and Bybit, using USDT as a stand-in for the dollar. It is not an official or guaranteed rate. The Central Bank of Nigeria official rate and bureau de change quotes are not included yet.

Main source: the free currency-api dataset (fawazahmed0). Live rates: Quidax, Binance P2P and Bybit P2P, collected by this site's rates server. Cross-check: Rates By Exchange Rate API. For information only, not financial advice.
